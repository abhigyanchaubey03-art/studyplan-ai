# StudyPlan AI

A free, rule-based study planner that turns your exam date and subjects into a personalized daily schedule — no sign-up, no backend, works entirely in your browser.

🔗 **Live site:** https://abhigyanchaubey03-art.github.io/studyplan-ai/

## Features

- **Plan** — enter your exam, exam date, daily study hours, and strong/weak subjects to generate a day-by-day schedule with revision blocks
- **Track** — a built-in timer to log real study sessions by subject
- **Progress** — daily/weekly totals, study streak, and a subject-wise breakdown

## How the plan is generated

- Weak subjects get 60% of your daily hours, strong subjects get 40%
- Subject order rotates each day so no subject is always last
- Each day includes a short revision block for a weak subject
- Weekly goals (practice questions, topics to revise) are calculated from your hours and inputs

## Tech stack

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript — no frameworks
- Hosted free on GitHub Pages
- Data (plans + study logs) is stored privately in your own browser
