Can I use StudyPlanAI for free?

Yes. The Start plan is designed to let students experience the core planner before deciding whether they need premium features.

What do I get with Pro?

Pro adds deeper practice, adaptive planning, smart revision, backlog recovery, advanced analytics and weekly progress reporting.

What is the difference between Pro and Elite?

Elite is designed as the complete preparation experience, adding the AI Study Coach, advanced test analysis, advanced backlog management, Preparation Score and Exam Mode.
