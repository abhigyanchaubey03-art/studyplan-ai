Frequently asked questions

Keep this section updated as your membership grows.

Can I use StudyPlanAI for free?

Yes. The Start plan is designed to let students experience the core planner before deciding whether they need premium features.

What do I get with Pro?

Pro adds deeper practice, adaptive planning, smart revision, backlog recovery, advanced analytics and weekly progress reporting.

What is the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro+ builds on everything in Pro, adding advanced AI recommendations, detailed test analysis, concept-level weakness detection, an exam-readiness score, an advanced revision system and priority AI features.

Can I cancel a paid membership?

Yes. Connect this page to your payment provider and account/subscription system so users can manage or cancel their subscription according to your published terms.

Will StudyPlanAI predict my rank or selection?

No. Any preparation score should be presented as a progress indicator, not a guarantee or prediction of exam rank, admission or selection.
