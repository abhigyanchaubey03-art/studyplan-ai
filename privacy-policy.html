Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

StudyplanAI ("we", "our", "the app") is a study planning and exam-prep tool for students. This page explains what information is collected, why, and how it's protected.

1. What we collect

  • Study plans, to-do items, and progress tracking: stored locally in your browser only. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.
  • Account information (if you sign in): when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and a unique account ID from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Study group data (if you join or create a group): your display name, which groups you're a member of, and study session summaries (subject and duration) you choose to log to a group, visible only to other members of that same group.
  • Membership status: whether you're on the free or a paid plan, and related subscription status.
Paid membership plans are planned but not yet live. When introduced, payment processing will be handled by a third-party payment processor (Razorpay) — we do not store your card or payment details ourselves; only limited transaction records (status, amount, and an external payment reference) are kept.

2. What we don't collect

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not access your personal study plans or to-do lists — they're stored only in your browser.

3. How your data is protected

Account and group data is stored with access controls that restrict each user to their own data, or to data shared within a group they've explicitly joined. Group join codes and rosters are not publicly accessible.

4. Your choices

  • You can use most features without signing in — study plans and tracking work entirely in your browser.
  • You can leave a study group at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).

5. Children and students

StudyplanAI is designed for exam-prep students, some of whom may be minors. We only collect the minimum information needed for sign-in and group features, and do not knowingly collect more than that.
